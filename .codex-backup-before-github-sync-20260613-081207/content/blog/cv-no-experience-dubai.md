# CV for Dubai With No Experience: First-Job Guide for 2026

You can make a strong CV even if you have no formal work experience. Employers in Dubai still want to see your education, training, skills, attitude, language ability, and availability. Your job is to show that you are ready to learn and reliable at work.

<!--cta-->

## Start with a clear objective

Use a short professional summary. Do not say only “I need a job.” Show what you can offer.

Example:

`Fresh graduate with strong communication skills, good English, basic computer knowledge, and a reliable attitude. Ready to learn quickly and support daily operations in a customer service or admin role. Available for immediate joining in Dubai.`

## Put education near the top

If you have no experience, your education can come before work history. Add school, course, year, and relevant projects.

You can also include training such as TESDA, online courses, food safety, basic computer training, first aid, or customer service certificates.

## Use internships, volunteering, and school projects

Experience does not only mean paid work. You can include:

- Internship
- OJT
- Volunteer work
- Family business support
- School projects
- Community events

Write what you did and what skills you used.

## Skills matter more when experience is limited

Add skills that match the job:

- Customer service
- MS Word and Excel
- Data entry
- Cash handling
- Cleaning and organizing
- Food safety
- Time management
- English communication

Do not add skills you cannot explain in an interview.

## Keep the CV simple

Use one page. Add a professional photo if the role is hospitality, retail, receptionist, or service-related. Include your phone with country code, email, location, visa status if relevant, and languages.
