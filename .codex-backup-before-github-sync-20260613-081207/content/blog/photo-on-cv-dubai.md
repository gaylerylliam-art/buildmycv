# Should You Put a Photo on Your CV in Dubai?

The honest answer is: it depends on the job. In Dubai and the UAE, CV photos are still common. Many recruiters expect them for customer-facing roles. But for some corporate and ATS-heavy roles, a photo is not required and may not help.

This guide will help you decide.

<!--cta-->

## When a CV photo can help

A photo can help for jobs where appearance, grooming, and customer interaction are part of the role. This includes hospitality, receptionist, retail, sales, customer service, front desk, beauty, and some domestic service jobs.

For example, a hotel recruiter may want to see that you look neat and presentable. A retail employer may want a friendly and professional image. In these cases, a good photo can support your application.

## When to skip the photo

You can skip the photo for roles where technical skills matter more than appearance. This includes software developer, IT support, finance analyst, accountant, engineer, and some multinational company applications.

If the company uses an ATS, your photo will not help the software read your CV. The ATS reads text. It cannot judge a photo. Your skills, job title, and keywords matter more.

## What a professional UAE CV photo looks like

Use a simple photo:

- Head and shoulders only
- Plain background
- Good lighting
- Formal or smart casual clothes
- Natural expression
- No filters
- No sunglasses
- No group photos

Your photo does not need to be expensive. It just needs to look clear and respectful.

## Common photo mistakes

Avoid selfies from cars, malls, bedrooms, or parties. Avoid cropped group photos where another person's shoulder is visible. Avoid old photos that no longer look like you.

For UAE roles, your photo should say one thing: this person is ready for work.

## Where to place the photo

Put the photo near your name and contact details. Do not make it too large. A small square or round photo is enough. Your CV content should still be the main focus.

## Final advice

If you apply for hospitality, retail, sales, reception, or service work in Dubai, include a good photo. If you apply for a multinational technical or finance role, the photo is optional. When unsure, use a clean ATS-friendly CV and keep the photo professional.
