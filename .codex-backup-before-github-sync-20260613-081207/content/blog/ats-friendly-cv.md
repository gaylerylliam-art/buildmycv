# What Is an ATS-Friendly CV? And How to Pass the Screening

ATS means Applicant Tracking System. It is software used by companies to collect, scan, and sort CVs. Before a recruiter reads your CV, the ATS may check if your document contains the right job title, skills, experience, and keywords.

An ATS-friendly CV is easy for software to read. It is also easy for humans to read.

<!--cta-->

## What ATS software checks

ATS tools usually check:

- Your job title
- Your work experience
- Skills that match the job posting
- Education and certifications
- Contact details
- Dates and employer names

If the job post asks for Excel, customer service, SAP, AutoCAD, DHA license, or UAE driving license, those words should appear naturally in your CV if they are true.

## Formatting rules

Use a clean layout. Avoid tables, text boxes, icons inside important sections, and images that contain text. The software may skip those parts.

Use standard headings:

- Professional Summary
- Work Experience
- Skills
- Education
- Certifications
- Languages

These headings help the ATS understand your CV.

## Keyword matching explained simply

If you apply for a Logistics Coordinator job, your CV should include relevant words such as logistics, shipment coordination, customs documents, WMS, inventory, supplier coordination, and Excel. Do not copy the job post word for word. Use the real skills and duties you have.

## Common ATS mistakes

Many applicants lose points because they use a beautiful design that the software cannot read. A CV with heavy graphics may look nice but perform badly. A simple CV with clear text often works better.

Also avoid writing everything in one big paragraph. Use short bullets under each job.
