# How to Write Your Visa Status on a CV in the UAE

In the UAE, visa status can affect hiring speed. Recruiters often want to know if you can join immediately, if you need a visa, or if you have a notice period. Writing visa status clearly can help employers decide faster.

<!--cta-->

## Where to place visa status

Place it near your contact details or in a small personal details section. Keep it short. Do not explain your full immigration history.

Good examples:

- Visa Status: UAE residence visa — transferable
- Visa Status: Visit visa — available immediately
- Visa Status: Employment visa — 30 days notice
- Visa Status: Spouse visa
- Visa Status: Golden visa

## When to include it

Include visa status when it helps the employer. If you are available immediately, say it. If you are already in Dubai, Abu Dhabi, or Sharjah, say your current location too.

For example:

`Dubai, UAE | Visit visa | Available immediately | +971 50 123 4567`

This line gives the recruiter useful information quickly.

## What not to include

Do not include passport number, visa number, Emirates ID number, or document scans in your CV. These are private details. Share documents only with a trusted employer at the correct stage.

## Notice period examples

If you are employed, write your notice period honestly:

- Notice Period: 30 days
- Notice Period: Immediate after cancellation
- Notice Period: 2 months as per contract

Do not write “immediate” if you cannot legally join immediately. Honesty protects you later.

## Final advice

Visa status is not always required, but in UAE recruitment it is often useful. Keep it short, clear, and professional.
