# Globe geometry

The land points and static image are generated from Natural Earth 1:110m land
geometry, which is public domain: https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/

Source: https://github.com/nvkelso/natural-earth-vector/blob/master/geojson/ne_110m_land.geojson

Regenerate with `node scripts/generate-globe-assets.mjs path/to/ne_110m_land.geojson`.
The illustration and motion are original; no LISRC assets are copied.
